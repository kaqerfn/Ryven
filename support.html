Getting Support

Before opening a ticket, make sure you provide enough information for our team to understand the issue.

  • Purchases Include your purchase information if your issue is related to a package.
  • Downloads Tell us which platform and build you're having trouble with.
  • Account Issues Explain the issue clearly and provide your Ryven username if necessary.
  • Other Issues Give as much information as possible so the support team can help you.
